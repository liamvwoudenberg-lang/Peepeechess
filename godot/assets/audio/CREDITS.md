# Audio credits

The sound effects and ambience loops in this folder (`*.ogg`, `sonniss_manifest.json`) are built from selected recordings of the **Sonniss #GameAudioGDC**
bundles (royalty-free, commercial use allowed, no attribution required). Slicing, pitch variants, loudness targets and loops are defined in
`tools/audio/build_sonniss.py`; rerun it to regenerate everything.

* The original raw WAV files are **not** in `main` (they are large, and the licence forbids redistributing the raw files). Keep them in
  `assets/audio_incoming/sonniss/` locally; that folder is git-ignored. The `audio-sounds` branch holds a copy.
* The shipped files are processed derivatives (short slices, resampled, normalised, encoded). Do not use them to train AI models (licence).
* The music score and the fallback sounds in `godot/assets/combat/audio` are generated by `tools/audio/generate_sfx.py` and the music director (no recordings).
